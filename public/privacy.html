Last updated: [date]

This DriveStream server is a private, personal streaming service run by [operator name] for themselves and invited family and friends. It is not offered to the public.

Google account access

The site owner can connect their own Google account so DriveStream can upload video files into the owner's Google Drive library folders. DriveStream uses this access only to create folders and upload files that the owner asks it to add, and to look up those folders to avoid duplicates. It does not read, share, sell or move any other Drive content.

The Google sign-in token is stored only on this DriveStream server and is never shared with anyone. It can be removed at any time with "Disconnect" in the dashboard, or from your Google account's third-party access settings.

Profiles and viewing history

Each viewer has a profile with a name, username and password (stored as a secure hash). DriveStream keeps each profile's playback positions so "Continue Watching" works. This data stays on this server and is not used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Third parties

Title information and artwork come from TMDB. Video files are stored in Google Drive or on this server's own disk. No analytics or advertising services are used.

Removing your data

Ask the site owner to delete your profile and its history at any time.
